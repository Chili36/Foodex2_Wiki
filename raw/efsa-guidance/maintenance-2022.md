# Maintenance 2022

<!-- Source: EFSA Supporting Publications - 2023 -  - FoodEx2 maintenance 2022.pdf p3, p11-16 -->
## Summary

- The biggest structural change was the addition of the PRIMo hierarchy with 1901 assigned terms. (2022 maintenance p3, p11)
- EFSA added 137 new terms for food-contact-material plasticiser work, avian influenza, chemical monitoring, and FAO/WHO GIFT requests. (2022 maintenance p3)
- The `Bird (as animal)` section was systematically revised, and 660 term descriptions were improved, especially in plants/botanicals. (2022 maintenance p3)

## Other Major Changes

- Plant references in the botanicals area were updated to World Flora Online. (2022 maintenance p3)
- The Feed hierarchy was revised under the updated Catalogue of Feed Materials: 33 new terms, 17 dismissed, and 17 other feed-term updates. (2022 maintenance p3)
- The maintenance also touched `F28 Process`, `F21 Production-method`, and amphibian/reptile fresh-meat sections. (2022 maintenance p4, p12-13)

## Release Context

- Covers MTX 13.1, 13.2 and the January 2023 major release MTX 14.0. (2022 maintenance p3)
