# Maintenance 2023

<!-- Source: EFSA Supporting Publications - 2024 - FoodEx2 maintenance 2023.pdf p3-4, p13-19 -->
## Summary

- EFSA added 306 new terms in 2023 for One Health surveillance, avian influenza, chemical monitoring, botanicals, additives/flavourings, and FAO/WHO GIFT requests. (2023 maintenance p3-4)
- A new facet hierarchy, `F34 Host-sampled`, was introduced. Two new implicit attributes, `VectorNetCode` and `ADDFOODCode`, were also created. (2023 maintenance p3, p16)
- For additives/flavourings monitoring, implicit legislative mapping was updated for 1988 terms. (2023 maintenance p3)

## Structural Changes

- Botanicals underwent a major revision: 97 new terms and 193 updates. (2023 maintenance p3)
- The Exposure hierarchy was reconstructed to 22 top-level groups, including a separate group for amphibians, reptiles, and terrestrial invertebrates. (2023 maintenance p4, p13)
- 4 terms were deprecated and 42 dismissed as redundant, duplicated, or unsuitable for the data used. (2023 maintenance p4)

## Release Context

- Covers MTX 14.1, 14.2, 14.3, 14.4 and the January 2024 major release MTX 15.0. (2023 maintenance p4)
