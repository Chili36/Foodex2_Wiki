# Maintenance 2015

<!-- Source: EFSA Supporting Publications - 2016 -  - FoodEx2 annual maintenance 2015.pdf p3, p7-20 -->
## Summary

- First post-revision-2 maintenance cycle. EFSA added 271 new terms across hierarchies and facets. (2015 maintenance p3)
- EFSA also clarified or expanded scopes, dismissed 3 duplicate terms, discouraged use of 9 inconsistent terms, and adjusted applicability and parent-child relationships. (2015 maintenance p3)
- A targeted revision aligned tea and herbal/fruit infusion sections with Regulation (EC) No 396/2005 and the THIE tea guidelines. (2015 maintenance p3, p15-17)

## Tea And Herbal-Infusion Boundary

- FoodEx2 normally treats a fresh plant product as raw and its dried form as a derivative. The marketed-dry exception instead treats spices and herbal infusion materials as raw commodities regardless of drying, because their fresh forms are rarely marketed in Europe. (2015 maintenance p15; EFSA revision 2 p42-43)
- The 2015 maintenance retained that exception for spices and herbal infusion materials but removed *Camellia sinensis* from it. Fresh *Camellia sinensis* leaves became raw commodity `A16GV`; dried or fermented tea leaves follow the standard derivative path. (2015 maintenance p15)
- `A04KK` was clarified as dried or fermented tea leaves from *Camellia sinensis*. It is not a general derivative base for non-tea herbal infusion materials. (2015 maintenance p13, p15)
- This boundary concerns dry material. A brewed infusion beverage and an herbal extract are separate product types and must not be collapsed into the dry-material base. (2015 maintenance p15-16)

## Why It Matters

- Term inventory and placement changed almost immediately after revision 2, even though the underlying logic stayed stable.
- Some apparent base terms were found inconsistent with system logic and should be replaced by a raw term plus process facet pattern. This is a separate issue from the marketed-dry exception above. (2015 maintenance p17-18)

## Release Context

- Maintenance window: 2015.
- Published as EFSA Supporting Publication 2016:EN-1049. (2015 maintenance p1-4)
