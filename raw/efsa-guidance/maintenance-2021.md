# Maintenance 2021

<!-- Source: EFSA Supporting Publications - 2022 -  - FoodEx2 maintenance 2021.pdf p3, p6-12 -->
## Summary

- EFSA added 91 new terms in 2021. Key additions served Avian Influenza, WGS, ASF, FAO/WHO GIFT, and smaller user requests. (2021 maintenance p3)
- 75 terms were dismissed in some hierarchies and 1 term was deprecated. Implicit facets were updated for 3 terms. (2021 maintenance p3)
- The `Birds (as animal)` section continued to expand, and `F02 Part-nature` gained 15 new terms for WGS and ASF-related coding. (2021 maintenance p3-4)

## Mapping Changes

- 1046 plant terms in `F01 Source` were mapped to EPPO classification. (2021 maintenance p3)
- Matrix-code attributes were updated for 256 terms in line with Regulation (EC) 2018/62. (2021 maintenance p3)
- These mapping passes also triggered parent-child revisions in plant and bird sections. (2021 maintenance p3)

## Release Context

- Covers MTX 12.1, 12.2 and the January 2022 major release MTX 13.0. (2021 maintenance p3)
