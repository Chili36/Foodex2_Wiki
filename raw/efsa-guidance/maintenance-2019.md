# Maintenance 2019

<!-- Source: EFSA Supporting Publications - 2020 - FoodEx2 maintenance 2019.pdf p3, p6-16 -->
## Summary

- EFSA added 587 new terms in 2019, many of them for avian-influenza bird species. (2019 maintenance p3)
- Existing terms were renamed or enriched with aliases and attributes; 2 terms were dismissed in some hierarchies, 2 deprecated as duplicates, and 1 deprecated for regulatory alignment. (2019 maintenance p3)
- Implicit facets were updated and some term types or hierarchy positions were changed for better parent-child logic. (2019 maintenance p3)

## Feed And Domain Changes

- The Feed hierarchy was heavily revised against the latest Catalogue of Feed Materials. (2019 maintenance p3, p9-11)
- New implicit attributes were added for feed provenance and mapping: `EUFeedReg`, OECD feed classification links, and IFN codes. (2019 maintenance p3)
- `F23 Target-consumer` was amended for more detailed feed-consumer classification. (2019 maintenance p3, p11)

## Release Context

- Covers MTX 10.1, 10.2, 10.3 and the January 2020 major release MTX 11.0. (2019 maintenance p3, p5)
