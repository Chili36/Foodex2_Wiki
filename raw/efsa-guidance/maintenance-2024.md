# Maintenance 2024

<!-- Source: EFSA Supporting Publications - 2025 -  - FoodEx2 maintenance 2024.pdf p3-5, p15-20 -->
## Summary

- EFSA added 1094 new terms in 2024, largely for One Health surveillance, avian influenza, chemical monitoring, and FAO/WHO GIFT requests. (2024 maintenance p3-4)
- Legislative mapping work continued in `F33 Legislative-classes`, and a new F33 category was introduced for VMPR and VMPR National Control Plan reporting. (2024 maintenance p3)
- The `VetDrugRes` hierarchy was substantially revised so its top level now mirrors food, feed, and non-food sections. (2024 maintenance p3, p15-16)

## Why This Is Important

- This is not just term growth. It changes how VMPR-related reporting is organised and mapped.
- The bird section in `F01 Source` was revised again, showing that avian-taxonomy support remains a recurring maintenance theme. (2024 maintenance p3)
- Reportability changed again: 1 term deprecated, 3 dismissed. (2024 maintenance p3)

## Release Context

- Covers MTX 15.1, 15.2, 15.3 and the January 2025 major release MTX 16.0. (2024 maintenance p4)
