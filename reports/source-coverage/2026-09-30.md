### COV-001: VMPR sheep/goat-feed exception is absent

**Resolved:** Added the explicit child requirement; all four targcon children verified in local MTX 17.2. A18EY corrects the duplicated goat label in the PDF.

Source: [EFSA Supporting Publications - 2026 - Chemical monitoring reporting guidance  2026 data collection.pdf](<../../foodex2_docs/EFSA Supporting Publications - 2026 - Chemical monitoring reporting guidance  2026 data collection.pdf>), PDF/printed page 36.

> it is always necessary to add one of the four codes grouped under the A07VF code.

**Observed:** The feed guidance allows implicit F23 but omits the specific requirement to refine implicit Sheep and goat feed (A07VF) with a child descriptor.

**Proposed action:** Add the scoped exception. Verify child codes against the catalogue: the source prints A18EX for two different goat labels, so do not copy that list blindly.

### COV-002: VMPR edible-casing instructions are absent

**Resolved:** Added casing base/F27 guidance and the adult-sheep example; code identities and memberships checked in MTX 17.2.

Source: [EFSA Supporting Publications - 2026 - Chemical monitoring reporting guidance  2026 data collection.pdf](<../../foodex2_docs/EFSA Supporting Publications - 2026 - Chemical monitoring reporting guidance  2026 data collection.pdf>), PDF/printed page 36.

**Proposed action:** Add the coding boundary and a catalogue-checked example, using the adult-sheep example on page 37 as source evidence.

### COV-009: Dismissed and deprecated terms are not distinguished accurately

**Resolved:** Clarified dismissal versus deprecation across rule, validation, ChemMon and maintenance pages; inspected sibling mapping and BR21 implementation, retaining blocking results.

Source: [EFSA Supporting Publications - 2021 -  - FoodEx2 maintenance 2020.pdf](<../../foodex2_docs/EFSA Supporting Publications - 2021 -  - FoodEx2 maintenance 2020.pdf>), PDF/printed page 3.

> Dismissed terms are made not reportable in one or more hierarchies of the FoodEx2 catalogue, but they continue to be reportable in others

**Observed:** The maintenance page mentions dismissal in some hierarchies, while validation guidance says deprecated and dismissed terms are always invalid without explaining the hierarchy scope. The source distinguishes dismissal from all-hierarchy deprecation.
